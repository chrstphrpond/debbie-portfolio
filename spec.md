## UX Constraints

- **Single mobile scroll** — fits on one phone screen session, no pagination
- **Max width**: 480px centered
- **Target device**: iPhone / Android via QR scan (mobile browser)
- **No login, no forms** — purely read-only
- **Accessible**: WCAG AA contrast minimums, readable font sizes (min 14px body)
- **Performance**: < 3s load on 4G, no heavy images until real photo supplied
